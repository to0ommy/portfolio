Overview

As a Research Assistant at the Integrative Biology and Microengineered Technologies (IBMT) Laboratory, I contributed to a tumour-on-a-chip platform under the mentorship of PhD student Han Shao and Professor Edmond Young. The platform models the tumour microenvironment in vitro.

Device fabrication & cell culture

My work included microfluidic channel design, PDMS casting, and device bonding.

I used sterile techniques to culture MDA-MB-231 breast cancer cells, GFP-A549 lung cancer cells, fibroblasts, and endothelial cells.

Imaging & analysis

I worked with brightfield and fluorescence microscopy and performed quantitative analysis of cell morphology and growth. The project explores a platform for studying tumour progression, cancer–stroma interactions, and therapeutic response.
